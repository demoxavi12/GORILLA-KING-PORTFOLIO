## Hero

## Final Creative Review

Ask:

1. Does it feel original?
2. Does it feel art-directed?
3. Is the animation purposeful?
4. Does typography carry enough of the design?
5. Are there quiet moments?
6. Does the site still work without the effects?
7. Does it feel like Sabya rather than a reference-site clone?
8. Would a visitor remember the experience?

If the answer to any major question is no, refine before declaring completion.
